# Update on IndiePass

As of January 2026, IndiePass is now looking for a new maintainer and is no longer available on either Google Play or the Amazon
Appstore. (The desktop and iOS versions of the app were previously archived in 2024)

This is due to a number of reasons, however the main one is due to increased legal requirements for keeping the app
available to comply with increasing online and child safety laws in various countries across the globe.

The app also requires a significant amount of updates to bring it up to par with modern Android standards and to ensure
it remains secure and compliant with upcoming requirements.

If you are interested in taking over the project, please [get in touch](mailto:mark@marksuth.dev).
